# TalentQuest

TalentQuest is a premium talent discovery, competition, judging and public voting platform.

## Product vision

Discover talent, showcase performances, combine judge scoring with public voting, and manage competition rounds from auditions through the grand finale.

## Planned core modules

- Public competition website
- Contestant applications and profiles
- Configurable talent categories
- Audition and performance video submissions
- Competition seasons and elimination rounds
- Judges and weighted scoring
- Public voting and Paystack payments
- Live/hidden leaderboards and results
- Admin, finance and reporting dashboards
- QR/shareable contestant voting pages
- Anti-fraud controls and vote/payment audit trail

## Brand

**TalentQuest**  
Discover • Vote • Support • Raise Stars

## Development

The project will be delivered in production-focused batches, using the proven architectural ideas from Face of Abuja while keeping TalentQuest independently extensible for talent competitions.
